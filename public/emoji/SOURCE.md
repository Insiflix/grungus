# Picker emoji artwork

Twemoji graphics by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (see LICENSE-GRAPHICS).
Data assembly is licensed under MIT (see LICENSE).

The unmodified, clean 32px sheet is bundled locally from:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/emoji-datasource-twitter@15.0.1/img/twitter/sheets-clean/32.png

Clean sheets contain no artwork from other vendors. Unsupported glyphs use the native Unicode fallback.
Sprite coordinates come from @emoji-mart/data 1.2.1, sets/15/twitter.json (61 columns and rows).
Regenerate coordinates with `node scripts/generate-emoji-sprites.mjs` after updating the matching sheet/data.

Upstream: https://github.com/twitter/twemoji and https://github.com/iamcal/emoji-data
